## Three ideas
1. **Before and after, one shape** (his Yishun case): a grey ring of 180 days, then a gold arc a sixth as long: sold in 1 month.
2. **Transformation chain** (his four promises): the gold ring becomes the target, the knight, the check, the house; the house becomes his card.
3. **Real-output wall:** his 30 "Just listed" video tours fly in depth and converge on his portrait.

Cover-the-logo test: 1 and 2 belong only to Nick (his case, his four promises, his icons, his face and number). 3 is real but busy and text-heavy.
**Pick: 1 as the hook and proof, flowing into 2 as the promise and the sign-off.** Carried object: the ring (time) becomes the gold line of his icons, then the house outline, then the card.
The current: leftward. Personality: premium / calm (property), snappy where it matters (the arc, the arrow, the press).

## Sound (SFX only)
Method: **Clipwing real-sfx** (github.com/Clipwing/skills): one recorded-sounding sound per cue, placed by its arrival on the exact frame, role peak levels (hit -4, whoosh -9, riser -12, click/pop -14 dBFS), a near-inaudible bed at -54 dBFS so the film never drops to digital silence, a mono check and a critique against reference SFX reels.
Sounds: **ElevenLabs official sound-effects skill** (github.com/elevenlabs/skills, model `eleven_text_to_sound_v3`), four takes per sound, picked by measurement. Five families: tick, whoosh, hit/thud, object foley (arrow, chess piece, pen, key), click/shimmer.
Silence: a 0.2 s dip before the hit on "Sold". Master: about -14 LUFS, true peak -1 dBTP or lower.
