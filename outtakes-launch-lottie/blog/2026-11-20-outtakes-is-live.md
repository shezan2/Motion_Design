# Outtakes is live: book a sports photographer in Singapore

Outtakes is a new website for finding and booking sports photographers in Singapore. It goes live on 20 November 2026 at 5PM SGT. Clubs, brands, players and parents can search one place, see each photographer's work and packages, pick a date and pay securely. Photographers get a proper page and get paid for every booking.

Most of us got into sports photography the same way. A friend's team needed someone on the sideline, we turned up with a camera, and a few weekends later people started asking what we charge. That's where the job stops being fun.

If you shoot football in Singapore, you know the chat. Someone asks how much for Saturday's match. You quote your rate, and they come back with half of it. The game gets called off the night before, and nobody mentions paying for the Saturday you kept free. Three weeks later you're still sending "just following up on the invoice :)" and getting left on Seen.

We're a collective of young sports photographers, mostly football, and every one of us has had that conversation more times than we can count. The two things we complained about most were chasing payments and not getting enough bookings. Outtakes is what we built to fix both.

## How to book a sports photographer in Singapore on Outtakes

Search for the sport you need, such as football, and browse the photographers who cover it. Each profile shows their featured shots, their packages with prices, and the dates they're free. When you've found someone, a booking takes four steps:

1. Call the photographer and agree on the shoot.
2. Book their package and date on Outtakes.
3. Pay once the photographer accepts.
4. Get shot, then get your photos.

[Find a photographer](https://outtakes.sg)

## Why sports photographers in Singapore are joining

Your rate is on your page. A client picks one of your packages at the price you set, which leaves a lot less room for the "can do $30?" message.

Payment goes through the platform. The client pays when you accept the booking, and if they cancel, you still get your cancellation fee. You don't have to chase anyone.

Invoices are generated for you. That helps most with clubs and companies, who usually need a proper invoice before they'll pay a freelancer.

Your portfolio sits in one organised place, with your featured shots, the sports you cover and your packages. You can send one link instead of a Google Drive folder.

Every week we also pick a Shot of the Week and feature it on our page. It costs you nothing, and it puts your photo in front of the clients who are browsing for someone to book.

It depends on the photographer and the shoot. On Outtakes, every photographer sets their own packages, and the price is shown on their profile before you book, so you can compare before you message anyone.

Search Outtakes for football photographers, check who is free on your match date, and book their package. You pay once they accept the booking.
