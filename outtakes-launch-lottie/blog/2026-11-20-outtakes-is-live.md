# We made a site for booking sports photographers in Singapore

Outtakes went live on 20 November 2026 at 5PM. It's a website for finding and booking sports photographers in Singapore, and you can see a photographer's work and prices before you ever message them.

We're a collective of young sports photographers, mostly football. The two things we complain about most are chasing payments and not getting enough bookings, and Outtakes is our attempt at fixing both.

If you've ever shot a match for someone, you've probably had the DM where they ask your rate and then ask if you can do it for $30. There are also the matches that get called off the night before, with nobody offering anything for the Saturday you kept free, and the clients who pay three weeks and a few "just following up on the invoice :)" messages later.

<!-- image: a full-width match photo. alt="Football player sprinting with the ball during a night match in Singapore" -->

## How to book a photographer for your match

Search for the sport you need and open a few profiles. Each one shows the photographer's best shots, their packages and prices, and the dates they're free, so you know what you're getting before you call them.

From there it's four steps:

1. Call the photographer and agree on the shoot.
2. Book their package and date on Outtakes.
3. Pay once they accept.
4. Get shot, then get your photos.

[Find a photographer](https://outtakes.sg)

## A few questions

### How much does a sports photographer cost in Singapore?

It depends on the photographer and what you need shot. Photographers on Outtakes set their own packages, and the price is listed on their profile before you book.

### Can I cancel a booking?

You can, but the photographer still gets their cancellation fee.

### Can my club or company get an invoice?

Yes, Outtakes can generate an invoice for the booking.

## If you're a photographer

Your packages and prices go on your page, and clients book them as they are. You'll still get the odd cheeky message, but it's a lot harder to haggle over a price that's already up there.

Clients pay through Outtakes once you accept a booking, and if they cancel, you still get your cancellation fee. For clubs and companies, Outtakes generates the invoice, so you're not building one in Word at midnight.

Your page doubles as your portfolio, so you've got one link to send people instead of a Google Drive folder. Clients searching Outtakes also see everyone on it, including photographers they'd never have come across on their own feed.

Each week we pick one photo as Shot of the Week and feature it on Outtakes, which gets the photographer some free exposure.
