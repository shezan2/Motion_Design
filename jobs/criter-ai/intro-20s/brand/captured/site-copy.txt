Questions
Frequently Asked Questions
What is CriterAI? [svg]
CriterAI is a legal audit engine backed by immigration professionals, providing pre-submission visa audits and assistance at a fraction of traditional professional fees.
It is designed to solve the “Migration Paradox”: the choice between expensive legal advice and risky DIY processes based on ungrounded information. By combining legal intelligence with expert review, CriterAI is designed to reduce AI hallucinations and help you identify issues before you submit your visa application.
What does CRITER stand for? [svg]
CRITER is an acronym for:
C — Cognitive
R — Reasoning
I — Intelligent
T — Technology
E — Evaluating
R — Regulation
.AI — Artificial Intelligence
Is CriterAI a replacement for a migration agent? [svg]
CriterAI is a self-service tool designed to make the visa process more accessible and affordable. While it doesn't replace professional legal advice, it provides expert-level guidance grounded in real immigration knowledge. Every AI response can be verified by a registered migration agent through our expert verification feature.
What is expert verification? [svg]
Every AI response in our chat assistant includes a "Verify by Expert" button. When you click it, a registered migration agent reviews the AI's answer, annotates each section as correct or incorrect, and provides a closing statement. You see the verified response with colour-coded highlights.
Which visa categories are supported? [svg]
During our closed beta, we support Education visas: Student Visa (Subclass 500), Student Guardian Visa (Subclass 590), Training Visa (Subclass 407), and Temporary Graduate Visa (Subclass 485). In the upcoming releases, we plan to expand to General Skilled Migration (Subclasses 189, 190, 491) and additional categories.
How much does it cost? [svg]
CriterAI is completely free during the closed beta period.
How accurate is the eligibility assessment? [svg]
Our assessments are powered by specialised AI models trained on real immigration expertise. While they provide strong guidance and confidence ratings, they are indicative, not a guarantee of outcome. We always recommend cross-checking with official sources, and you can use our expert verification feature for added confidence.
Is my data secure? [svg]
Absolutely. Your data is encrypted both at rest and in transit (AES-256 and TLS 1.3). We comply with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and follow industry-standard security practices.
