## PAGE
- Problem: Australian visas mean confusing options and paperwork; professional advice is expensive and generic AI guesses (their "Migration Paradox").
- Audience: international students and migrants planning an Australian visa; website, YouTube, LinkedIn.
- Goal: start for free at criter.ai.
- Emotion: relief, then trust.
- The one true thing: instant AI visa guidance that a registered migration agent can verify, in as little as 30 minutes.

## Three ideas
1. Chaos to order with the logo's check (pick). Their own subclass marquee becomes the noise ("Which visa? Which documents? What's next?"), collapses into the gold check, the check opens the chat, an answer gets "Verify by Expert", the rows tick, payoff line, logo.
2. UI documentary of their four steps (Upload, Profile, Assess, Apply) for one student.
3. Typographic essay on the Migration Paradox: expensive advice or a risky guess, then the third way.

Pick: 1, using the Verify by Expert press as the hero proof. The C+check is CriterAI's own mark and the expert check is what generic AI cannot claim (cover-the-logo test passes). Carried object: the gold check, then the word "AI". Current: up.

## Sound plan (SFX only)
Families: UI clicks/ticks/pops, air (whoosh, swell, riser, swish), the check chime (the brand's motif, reused on every check), low warm hits/thuds, textures (keyboard, clock ticks).
Skills used: elevenlabs/skills `sound-effects` (SFX v3 via the ElevenLabs connector) for designed sounds; GhazaGG/Game-Sound-Effect-for-Claude (`game-sfx`) synth for tuned chimes and ticks, and its `doctor.py` on every file; AbubakrChan/product-launch-motion's rule of placing each sound by its measured peak on cues exported from the film. Mix to -14 LUFS, true peak -1 dBTP or lower, room tone floor, no silent tail.
