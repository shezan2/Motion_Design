 FAQ
## Common questions
 What exactly is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?
GEO is the practice of optimizing your brand so it gets cited and recommended by AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity. While traditional SEO gets you to the top of Google, GEO ensures AI tools mention your brand when customers ask for recommendations.
 How is OmniGro AI different from other AI visibility tools?
We're a done-for-you service, not just a tracking tool. We take time to understand your specific brand, audience, and competitive landscape before building a custom GEO strategy. We don't apply the same playbook to every client; we craft a unique approach for each brand.
 How quickly will I see results?
Our pilot runs 8 weeks. Early results typically show within 2-3 weeks of launch. In our latest pilot with Three Squared Nine, organic clicks grew 451% and search impressions grew 2,778% by the end of the pilot.
 What results has OmniGro achieved for clients?
In our latest pilot with Three Squared Nine, a fractional legal counsel based in Singapore, we delivered +451% organic clicks, +2,778% search impressions, and took them from zero AI visibility to #1 listings on ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity. Unbranded AI discovery went from 0% to 58-74% visibility scores.
 What does the free AI visibility report include?
The free report shows you exactly where your brand currently stands on ChatGPT and Gemini. You'll see your current citation rate, which AI platforms mention you, and where your competitors are beating you. We also audit your website and tell you what AI crawlers can and can't read. See this post for the full breakdown on what's included.
 What types of brands do you work with?
We work with consumer brands across industries: CPG, fintech, media, B2B services, professional services, and more. If your customers use AI to research products or services, we can help.
 What happens after the pilot?
After the pilot, you receive a comprehensive results report and a done-for-you action plan for long-term AI visibility. Ongoing monthly plans start at US$1,500 a month, on a 6-month or 12-month term.
 Is my data secure?
We design with privacy in mind from day 1. Data is isolated per client, and we have clear data retention and deletion controls.
 Does OmniGro AI also offer SEO?
Yes, and SEO and GEO are highly complementary. There's a huge overlap between the two: strong content, authoritative backlinks, and clear brand signals help you rank on Google and get cited by AI alike. For businesses that want both, we handle SEO and GEO together, optimizing holistically for growth and helping you reach your audience wherever they search, whether on traditional search engines or AI assistants.
