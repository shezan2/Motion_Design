Made with the ozen-motion-design skill, as an Ozen Flow production (Ozen's motion design service).

## Brief (24 Nov 2026)
"Using all the motion design references and the skill, create the Outtakes platform onboarding video from scratch for Ozen Flow."
- Assumed: about 39 s; 9:16 master plus 16:9; synthesised SFX (no licensed music on hand); no voiceover in this cut (Shezan's working preference: picture and SFX first, voice later).
- Facts used (all from the site recordings of 23 Nov): the Studio's 8-step checklist and its 10-step tour copy; the profile page; "Get in the frame"; photographer onboarding from 24 Nov, photographers only, free to list.
- Illustrative: "Your name", the bio, the package (Full match, 2 hrs, 60 photos, ready in 3 days, S$70), the booking request (Sat 28 Nov), the DM lines (approved in the launch film).

## Brand sheet (Outtakes site, v4)
- Grounds: near-black #0f0f11 with the site's green glow rising from the bottom (dark acts); light grey #e5e9ec (the Studio act). Cards #f4f6f7, panels #d8dde1.
- Ink #121214; brand green #4bff2c (text on light #1f9e10); Studio orange #f77128 (tour dashes, Next, progress).
- Type: OT Wordmark (condensed black italic caps) for statements; OT Display for small titles; Archivo body.
- Shapes: the tour card (white, orange dashes, orange Next), the green "Join as Photographer" pill, film strip, focus brackets.
- Voice: short, sporty, confident. Never: stock imagery, invented reviews (the site's reviews are demo data: not shown).

## PAGE
- Problem: great shots, messy bookings (photos scattered, deals made in DMs).
- Audience: Singapore sports photographers, on Instagram and TikTok.
- Goal: join as a photographer and set up the Studio.
- Emotion: "this is easy, and it gets me booked".
- One true thing: the Studio walks you through setup once; then clients find you, book and pay on the site.

## Ideas
1. UI documentary of the real Studio tour (1-10 of 10), the card morphing into each feature.
2. Chaos to order: scattered shots and DMs converge into one Join button.
3. Typographic essay on "Get in the frame".
Pick: 2 as the hook into 1, ending on a booking (the tour's own "Bookings: requests land here" paid off), then "Get in the frame". Covers-the-logo test: the tour copy, orange Studio and green action are Outtakes' own.
Carriers: the green pill (chaos converges into it, its press floods into the Studio), the tour card (morphs into every feature), the cursor, the Accept button (its green bloom flips the film back to dark).
Current: content leaves left, the next card arrives from the right.
