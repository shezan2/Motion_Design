# Outtakes / photographer onboarding / v3

An Ozen Flow production, made with the ozen-motion-design skill.

**Idea:** the photographer's mess (shots everywhere, deals in DMs) converges into one green button; pressing it opens the Studio, and the Studio's own 10-step tour, carried by a single card that grows into each feature, takes them from "Welcome" to "BOOKED." Only Outtakes could run this film: it is their tour copy, their orange Studio, their green action and their "Get in the frame."

**Beats (what each one buys):**
1. TOO MANY SHOTS. / DMS.: the viewer recognises their own chaos.
2. The chaos converges into "Join as Photographer": there is one place for all of it.
3. SET IT UP ONCE. (Welcome, Bookings): setup is guided and quick to start.
4. THE PAGE CLIENTS SEE. (Your profile): what clients will see.
5. SIX SHOTS. TAGGED BY SPORT. (Portfolio): the scattered shots get organised.
6. SET YOUR PRICE. (Packages): you name the price; clients pay on the site.
7. MARK YOUR FREE DAYS. (Availability): clients can find you for their date.
8. PAID WHEN PHOTOS ARRIVE. (Payouts): payment is handled.
9. ALMOST THERE. / ALL SET. (the rest of the tour, the checklist at 8 of 8): it is finite and you finish it.
10. A request lands, Accept, BOOKED.: the payoff the tour promised ("requests from clients land here").
11. GET IN THE FRAME. / OUTTAKES / outtakes.sg: the call to action.

**Real:** every tour card's title and copy (the Studio, recorded 23 Nov 2026), the 8 checklist steps and their "recommended" tags, the Studio's orange UI, the profile page layout, "Get in the frame", Earnings this month S$0.00, the DM lines (approved in the launch film), photographer onboarding from 24 Nov.

**Illustrative:** "Your name", the avatar, the bio, the package (Full match, 2 hrs, 60 edited photos, ready in 3 days, S$70), the chosen days (26, 28, 29 Nov), the booking request (Sat 28 Nov), the profile's details. The site's demo reviews are deliberately left out.

**Sound:** synthesised: shutters, pops, ticks, taps, whooshes, chimes, an autofocus beep, impacts; the crowd under the hook, a soft two-chord pad under the Studio and a brighter chord from BOOKED. No licensed music and no voiceover in this cut.

Mean motion 2.2/255, below the human band (4.4-9.8): a light, UI-led film with reading holds; every hold over a second has a living element (the drifting ground light, a camera push, a sweep, the cursor).

**Frames (2 fps review):** shots tumble into a dark pile under TOO MANY SHOTS., DM bubbles pop, the keyword swaps to DMS.; everything converges into a green dot that becomes Join as Photographer; the press floods into the light Studio; SET IT UP ONCE. over the Welcome card, then Bookings; each later step arrives from the right and grows into its feature (profile header, a 3x2 portfolio with sport tags and stars, the package with S$70 typed and Active on, the November calendar with 26, 28 and 29 tapped, Payouts connected), the camera pushing toward the detail; ALMOST THERE. over steps 8 to 10; ALL SET. as the checklist ticks to 8 of 8; Preview profile cuts to the page; a booking request drops in; Accept blooms green into the dark; BOOKED.; GET IN THE FRAME.; OUTTAKES locks into focus with outtakes.sg.
