# Worked example: Ozen's own launch ad (30 s)

A full plan made by following this skill on a one-line brief: "an ad for Ozen's motion design launch, 30 seconds". Use it as the shape of a good plan, not as a template to copy. Facts come from the rate card; anything assumed is marked.

## Brief, as received
Ad for the motion design service. Audience: Singapore SMEs and agencies. 30 s, 9:16 first, plus 1:1 and 16:9. Music and SFX; voiceover optional. Assumed: no launch discount (none was given, so none is shown).

## Brand sheet
- Ground: near-black navy #0a0b14 to #12162e. Ink: warm white. Accent: electric-blue gradient #2b3ff0 to #1a2bb8 for the hero card; orange-red #ff4d2e only for savings and the final call to action.
- Type: a clean grotesk for everything, a serif italic for one accent word ("Motion design *videos*").
- Shapes: the ÖZEN wordmark; the bezier curve with two handles from the top of the rate card.
- Core gesture: easing, a thing starting to move.
- Voice: short, confident, friendly. Never: stock footage, hype words, invented clients.

## PAGE
- Problem: SME posts are static graphics that get scrolled past; agencies want motion they can resell without hiring.
- Audience: SME owners and marketers in Singapore, agency leads; Instagram and LinkedIn.
- Goal: order a first video (from S$99) or start a monthly plan.
- Emotion: "that could be my brand", then "that's easy and affordable".
- The one true thing: branded motion videos, first draft in 2 working days, from S$99.

## Three ideas
1. **The curve gives things motion** (transformation chain): the rate card's bezier curve is the protagonist. It catches a dead static post and pulls it into motion, then becomes every seam: the timeline, the playhead, the underline of the wordmark.
2. **Static vs moving** (before and after): the same post twice in a feed, once still and scrolled past, once animated and stopping the thumb.
3. **Brief to posted** (UI documentary): a WhatsApp brief arrives, a draft renders, a comment, the final video goes live on a phone.

Pick: 1, opening with the before/after beat from 2 and using the four steps of 3 as its proof. Why: the curve is Ozen's own shape (it's on the rate card), and the film demonstrates the product by being motion design.

Carried object: the bezier curve and its end handle (a small square), which becomes the playhead dot and finally the line under the wordmark. The current: content exits left, the curve draws left to right.

## Formats
- 9:16 master as above.
- 1:1: beats 4-7 keep one card at a time; the plan beat shows the Growth card alone, then a single line for Starter and Pro.
- 16:9: the plan beat shows three cards in a row with Growth at twice the area of the others; the claims run before the proof, never beside it.
