# Reference reels: the house library

Shezan sent 11 iPad screen recordings of Instagram reels on 24 Nov 2026 as "excellent motion design" references. They are 8 distinct pieces (some recordings continue the same reel). Numbers come from `scripts/ref_breakdown.py`; descriptions from the contact sheets. These are other creators' work: learn the mechanics and timing, never reuse their frames, brand assets or copy.

## 1. What the eight have in common

| Measure | Continuous pieces (7) | Hype montage (1) |
|---|---|---|
| Hard cuts | 1-3 per 15-20 s (scenes morph instead) | 21-27 in 16 s |
| Average shot length | 2-8 s | 0.6-0.75 s |
| Big move length | median about 5 frames @30 (2-13 f); morphs run 15-30 f | 1-2 f |
| Something new happens | every 0.7-1.2 s (0.3-1.5 s) | every 0.27 s |
| Reading holds | several per piece, 0.5-1.4 s each | 1-2 s at the end |
| Frames with visible motion | 26-70% | 28% |
| Words per beat | 2-6 | 0-5 |
| Ending | logo resolves from the motif, short hold, or a loop back to frame 1 | small logo on black |

About half the pieces put their big moves on sound onsets well above chance (Ava AI 92% against 50% by chance, youmotion 64% against 28%, OpenAI part two 79% against 53%). The rest are near chance, so the sound was cut to the picture rather than the other way round.

Shared traits:
- One accent colour on a quiet ground (white + green, cream + orange, black + blue).
- Type carries the message; it is small to medium, centred and sentence case, with one accented word.
- Product UI is simplified but holds real-looking content, and a cursor or press drives the action.
- The problem is visual noise; the product is order. Lines, dots and glyphs converge into the brand.
- A payoff word gets a hold, then the brand resolves from the film's own motif.
- Three of the eight loop: the last frame leads back into the first, so a replay looks seamless (good for Reels watch time).

## 2. The eight pieces

### 2.1 calbby.mg: "Still looking for an editor" (recording r01)
Self-promotion for a video editor. 9:16 with central panels. About 13 s, loops.
- 0.6 s blue shards fly in -> 1.6 "Still looking for an (editor)" blurs into focus, "editor" inside an outlined pill, blue horizon glow -> 3.0 grey ground: a search bar materialises, browser buttons pop around it, "Calbby.mg" types in -> 6.0 the search bar inflates into a profile card (avatar, name, "I make pictures move", coloured tag chips) on a lilac radial glow -> 7.6 "Behind The Cuts" thumbnails, the cursor clicks one -> 8.3 dolly into darkness, 3D cards fan, "Get quality edits" -> 9.6 bold purple stripes "Calbby / request PROJECT", a card spins -> 10.6 a line inflates into a blue chat bubble "Let's work together" -> 12.3 "Calbby" whips past with motion blur -> the opening shards again.
- Numbers: 4 hard cuts, ASL 2.7 s, long morphs (median move 15-18 f), a beat every 1.2 s.
- Borrow: typing the brand into a search bar as the hook; a container morph (search bar -> profile card); a chat-bubble CTA; the seamless loop.
- Watch: the colour temperature changes almost every scene and only holds together because each change is carried by a morph.

### 2.2 divyansh.fx: "Attention is expensive" (r02 + r03)
A faceless kinetic-type brand piece. 9:16, white, one green, black. About 29 s, loops.
- "Attention" -> "Attention is expensive" (words blur in, small $ particles, the key word in green) -> push into a giant clock dial, "Because every second" (serif italic in green) -> whip to "Your audience spends" with a crowd of person icons, one black figure inside viewfinder brackets -> whip to "Trying to understand ???" with testimonial cards drifting around it -> zoom blur to a ring counter "8 -> 13 Is a second" -> the ring collapses to a dot -> the dot grows into a hexagon "Losing prospects" with a callout line; a honeycomb builds ("Losing momentum", "Losing opportunities") -> one word at a time: "The / brands / the / market." -> "Aren't louder." in black serif italic -> pill chips cascade in from both sides, alternating filled green and outlined: "They're clearer", "Sharper", "More intentional", "They remove friction", "They accelerate understanding", "They make decisions feel effortless" -> everything defocuses away -> "Because clarity isn't an advantage" types on -> the sentence slides left, "It's leverage." is circled by a hand-drawn green ellipse -> the logo decodes from scrambled letters -> "Attention" (loop).
- Numbers: 1-2 hard cuts in 29 s; reading holds 0.7-1.1 s; a beat every 1.2 s in the first half, every 2.6 s in the chip section (slow on purpose: lists need reading time).
- Borrow: the sans + serif-italic accent pair; dot -> ring -> hexagon as the carrier; a ring counter; a chip cascade for benefit lists; an ellipse around the payoff; a scramble-decode logo; the loop.
- Watch: it lives on copy. Without a sharp script it is a slideshow. "Isn't X. It's Y." works once per film, not as a pattern.

### 2.3 anmoldoesmotion: OpenAI concept ad, "Had an idea. Made it move" (r04 + r05)

- Tiny "< too many signals >" in a black field with hairline crosshairs -> code fragments drift around "too many tabs" -> "too many thoughts" glows -> a blue point emits a sine wave that splits into fibres -> the fibres reach UI chips ("12 new messages", "research_final_v4", "meeting at 5pm", "image_prompt.txt", "app.py") -> the lines collapse into dots on a circle -> a black disc with a glowing blue ring, "connected.", on silver -> the camera flies through the rings into white -> "OpenAI" reveals letter by letter in boxes, the tracking opens and it fades -> beach photo, a glass prompt box: "What will we build?", typing "Design typography posters that stop scrolling" -> push into the send button, press -> typographic posters burst out radially -> "Seconds." -> a code editor over misty forest types itself -> the ground flashes red: one line errors with a cross, "Fixing issue..." -> fixed, green ticks -> a 3D voxel cube renders beside the code -> a beige field of floating labels (Feedback, Clients, Requests...) with depth of field -> an illustrated branch grows and connects them, leaves fall -> sky and flowers: "One place to Imagine / Build / Discover." - Numbers: 7-8 hard cuts per half (ASL about 2 s); snappy moves (median 4-5 f); a beat every 0.55-1.1 s.

- Watch: it visits many worlds (black, silver, white, beach, forest, beige, sky) and survives on one carrier and one blue. Too much for a 15 s Starter video.

## 3. Which piece to study for which job

| Job | Study | Why |
|---|---|---|
| SaaS or app explainer | Zapier (2.8), Ava AI (2.7), OpenAI (2.3) | prompt and workflow UI, chaos to order, honest demo beats |
| Product or feature launch | Apple Widgets (2.4), OpenAI (2.3) | "Introducing", UI constellation, count-ups, resizing demo |
| Brand or manifesto reel, copy-led | divyansh.fx (2.2) | kinetic type, serif accent, chips, loop |
| Logo sting or opener (3-10 s) | youmotion (2.5) | carrier dot, shape morphs, blobs to logo |
| Personal brand or service business | calbby.mg (2.1) | search bar hook, profile card, chat CTA |
| Hype teaser or event | BLACKBOX.AI (2.6) | one treatment over fast footage, persistent caption |
| Ozen's own portfolio posts | Ava AI frame (2.7) | video in a card with the chapter bar shows the craft |

## 4. How to use references with clients
- Run `scripts/ref_breakdown.py` on whatever the client sends, look at every sheet, and write one row per reference: what it is, its structure, its two or three signature moves, its numbers.
- Tell the client in plain words what you will take from each ("the way the logo forms from the chaos in the Zapier ad, the chip list from the attention reel"). It shows craft and sets expectations.
- Mix at least three sources, then re-skin everything in the client's brand: fonts, colours, materials and the carried object come from the client.
- If a reference needs footage, 3D or licences the plan can't cover, say so and offer the nearest move from `moves.md` that fits the budget.
