# Shezan's verdicts

What Shezan has actually said about videos and copy made for Ozen and its clients. These are reactions to specific work: generalise each only as far as its context. Read this before choosing an idea, and append a line whenever Shezan reacts to new work: date, the work, the words used, what they were about, the narrowest lesson.

## Wants
- **2026-11, Outtakes launch film:** "make it look extremely professional and like a million dollar video", "using best Advertisement references out there". Lesson: ad-grade craft is the bar for every client film: one case followed end to end, a carried object, magnified 3D UI, motivated seams, designed sound.
- **2026-11, Outtakes:** "ensure the some of the cool animations from the website are also included". Lesson: borrow the client's own signature motion (their site's hover states, wipes, scrolls) so the film feels like their product.
- **2026-11, Outtakes:** "design the video specifically for portrait". Lesson: 9:16 is composed first, not adapted from 16:9.
- **2026-11, voiceover:** "either a very womanly woman's voice for the video or a mans deep voice". Lesson: the two house voices in `sound.md`.
- **2026-11, many requests:** "i need something i can copy paste". Lesson: deliver prompts, scripts and messages as ready-to-paste blocks.
- **2026-11-24, references:** eleven reels sent as "excellent" motion design (`reference-reels.md`). Lesson: the target look is the continuous-take SaaS and brand style: morphs instead of cuts, small precise kinetic type, one accent colour, glow as material, UI as the hero, the logo resolving from the motif.
- **2026-11, Ozen rate card ("this is what we went with in the end"):** near-black navy with an electric-blue gradient card, one orange-red accent for savings, a grotesk with a serif-italic accent word ("Motion design *videos*"), the bezier curve with handles. Lesson: this is Ozen's own look for Ozen's own videos and posts.

## Rejected
- **2026-11, Outtakes first cuts:** "its a bit too fast paced btw slow it down a bit". Lesson: give the lines that matter longer holds and the film room to breathe; keep moves snappy rather than slowing everything.
- **2026-11, Outtakes portrait version:** crops that cut the subject. Lesson: crop every format around a stored subject point and re-check every frame.
- **2026-11, Outtakes v4:** "that last scene is the website footer looks very awkward". Lesson: no web-footer end cards; end on a short, designed lockup.
- **2026-11, Outtakes v4:** "remove that glowing live circle before the live word at the end". Lesson: no fake live indicators; copy must match the real state (photographers-only onboarding from 24 Nov).
- **2026-11, voiceover takes:** whispered lines too quiet; "remove the noise in the background"; "this voice doesn't sound good"; "i dont want a singaporean voice". Lesson: level-match lines, no ambience bed under a voice, neutral accent, test a short line before a full take.
- **2026-11, blog copy:** "still sounds ai written". Lesson: any copy longer than a line gets a humanizer pass and real phrasing.

## Working preferences
- Make the picture and SFX cut first; the voiceover comes later as a separate version ("remove voiceover ill add a new one later").
- Launch facts change late (Outtakes moved from 16 to 20 to 22 to 24 November): keep dates and prices as constants in one place so a change is one edit and one partial re-render.
