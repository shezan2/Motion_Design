The Protocol

One circuit. Three rounds.

The sequence regulars swear by — the same circuit athletes run before competition. Simple to remember, hard to skip once you've felt the difference.

Scan on arrival 2 minutes

Step on the scanner before anything else. Your body composition is logged so every visit builds a picture of your progress.

Cryotherapy completely dry, before heat or water

Cryo works best on dry skin, so it comes first. Three minutes at −85°C — total reset for circulation and mind.

Sauna 15 minutes

Move into the dry heat. Fifteen unhurried minutes — enough for a real sweat and for muscle tension to genuinely release.

Ice bath 2–4 minutes

Straight from the sauna into 5°C water. Control the breath, settle in, and find calm in the cold.

Repeat × 3 rounds

The magic is in the cycling. Run the sauna-to-ice-bath contrast three times — each round gets easier and lands deeper.

Thermal pool wind down

Finish warm. Relax in every ripple of the 38°C thermal pool before you rejoin the world.

This is a guide, not a prescription. Listen to your body, hydrate throughout, and step out of any station the moment something feels wrong. First-timers should start with shorter rounds — the team will guide you.
